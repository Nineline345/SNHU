### How do I approach designing software?

My approach to designing software starts with understanding the requirements and breaking the project into smaller, manageable components. For my CS 330 project, I selected a photograph of my computer workstation and identified the basic 3D shapes needed to recreate it. This included using boxes for the monitors and keyboard, cylinders and toruses for the soda can, and a plane for the desk. I learned how important it is to consider object positioning, proportions, textures, and lighting during the design process. In future projects, I can apply this same approach by planning the individual components before combining them into a complete application.

### How do I approach developing programs?

I approach development by implementing functionality in stages, testing each change, and making adjustments when necessary. Throughout this course, I became more comfortable working with C++, OpenGL, transformations, textures, shaders, and camera controls. Each milestone introduced additional functionality to my existing project, which allowed me to build on previously completed work instead of starting over. Iteration played an important role, particularly when adjusting the lighting and wood texture to achieve the appearance I wanted. I also learned the importance of testing changes before moving forward. This reinforced my preference for incremental development and helped me better understand how individual components work together.

### How can computer science help me in reaching my goals?

My goal is to continue developing my programming skills and eventually work professionally in software development or an adjacent role which relies upon my computer science knowledge, such as a manufacturing systems analyst. This course introduced me to an area of computer science that I had limited experience with before. Learning how to create and manipulate 3D environments helped me better understand graphics rendering, coordinate systems, and interactive applications. While I do not necessarily plan to specialize in computer graphics, I can see these skills being useful in simulations, data visualization, and software that requires interactive interfaces. The experience also strengthened my understanding of C++ and reinforced the importance of writing organized, functional code. As I continue my education and career, I hope to apply these development practices to increasingly complex software projects.
